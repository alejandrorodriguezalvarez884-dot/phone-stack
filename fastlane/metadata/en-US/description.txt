Phone Stack turns the classic dinner phone-stack game into an app: everyone puts their phone face down on the table, and the first one to pick theirs up pays.

HOW IT WORKS
• One person creates a table and shares the 4-character code. Everyone else joins with it.
• The host starts the round and everyone places their phone face down.
• When every phone is face down and still, a short beep tells the table the round is live.
• The moment someone picks their phone up, leaves the app or loses connection, the alarm goes off on every phone and their name appears with a random penalty.

FEATURES
• Real-time sync between all the phones at the table.
• Uses the motion sensor to tell when a phone is face down and when it is picked up.
• Scoreboard that keeps count of who has lost the most rounds.
• Penalties to make it fun: pay for the coffees, give a toast, clear the table…
• Solo practice mode to try the sensor before dinner.
• No account, no ads, no tracking. Just type your name and play.
• Available in English and Spanish.

Less scrolling, more talking. Put your phone down and enjoy dinner.
